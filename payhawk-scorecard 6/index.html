Scoring Guide

Methodology reference and full scoring criteria for all six dimensions

Methodology

SPIN Selling
Situation → Problem → Implication → Need-Payoff. Use in the first half of every discovery to uncover latent pain before presenting Payhawk. Listen 70%, talk 30%.
Challenger Sale
Teach → Tailor → Take Control. Re-frame the partner's view of their own opportunity cost. Deploy once you have enough context to deliver a commercial insight that shifts their perspective.
MEDDPICC
Metrics · Economic Buyer · Decision Criteria · Decision Process · Paper Process · Identify Pain · Champion · Competition. Use throughout to qualify partnership fit — escalate toward close.

Scoring Tiers

22–30
PRIORITISE
Fast-track to partnership agreement. Target first client introduction within 30 days. Book a QBR cadence.
14–21
DEVELOP
Build a 90-day nurture plan with defined milestones. Identify exactly which dimension is lowest and address it first.
6–13
DEPRIORITISE
Park with light-touch content nurture (monthly newsletter, event invites). Mark for re-evaluation in 6 months.
